# Personal Protective Equipment Standard

## Mandatory site PPE

The minimum PPE for all persons on site is: safety helmet compliant with AS/NZS 1801, high visibility clothing, steel capped or composite safety boots, and safety glasses. Additional PPE is task based and determined by the SWMS or safe operating procedure for the activity.

## Hearing protection

Hearing protection is mandatory in any signposted hearing protection zone and for any task where noise exposure exceeds 85 dB(A) averaged over eight hours, or a peak of 140 dB(C). Where earmuffs are used with a safety helmet, they must be a compatible helmet mounted type.

## Respiratory protection

Task based respiratory protection is required for silica dust generating activities such as cutting, grinding or drilling concrete and masonry. Half face P2 respirators are the minimum for intermittent work; powered air purifying respirators are required for extended dry cutting tasks. Workers using tight fitting respirators must be clean shaven at the face seal and must have passed a fit test within the last twelve months.

## Helmet replacement

Safety helmets must be replaced no later than three years from date of issue, or immediately after any significant impact, whether or not damage is visible. The date of issue must be written on the inside of the shell.

## Gloves

Cut resistant gloves rated to at least level C are required for tasks with laceration risk, including glass handling, sheet metal work, and use of open blades. Gloves must not be worn near rotating plant such as drill presses and lathes, where entanglement is the greater hazard.
