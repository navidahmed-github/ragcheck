# Excavation and Trenching Permit Requirements

## Permit to dig

No ground may be broken on site without a valid permit to dig issued by the site engineer. The permit application must include the proposed excavation boundary, expected depth, the method of excavation, and evidence that underground services have been located.

Permits are valid for a maximum of seven days and must be renewed if conditions change, including after heavy rainfall or any nearby ground movement.

## Underground service location

Before a permit is issued, current service plans must be obtained through the Before You Dig service, and services within the excavation zone must be positively located by potholing with non-destructive methods such as vacuum excavation. Locating by plans alone is not sufficient for mechanical excavation within one metre of a marked service.

## Trench collapse controls

Any trench deeper than 1.5 metres must be benched, battered, or supported by shoring or trench shields before entry. Soil type must be assessed by a competent person, and spoil must be placed at least one metre back from the trench edge. Ground support requirements must be recorded on the permit.

## Trench inspection

A competent person must inspect the trench and its ground support at the start of every shift, after any rainfall, after any unplanned ground movement, and before re-entry following any period where the trench was left unattended. Inspection results must be recorded on the daily excavation checklist attached to the permit.

## Access and egress

Safe access and egress, such as ladders or ramps, must be provided within nine metres of any worker in a trench. Ladders must extend at least one metre above the trench edge and be secured against movement.
