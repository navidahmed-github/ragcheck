# Electrical Safety on Site

## Testing and tagging

All portable electrical equipment, leads and RCDs used on a construction site must be inspected, tested and tagged by a competent person every three months. The tag colour changes each quarter (red, green, blue, yellow) so out-of-date equipment is visible at a glance. Untagged or out-of-date equipment must be removed from service immediately.

## Residual current devices

All portable electrical equipment must be connected through a residual current device (RCD). Portable RCDs must be push-button tested by the user each day before use, and time/current tested as part of the quarterly test and tag cycle.

## Leads and boards

Electrical leads must be kept off the ground on insulated lead stands or hooks, kept clear of water, and never run through doorways where they can be damaged. Double adaptors and piggyback plugs are prohibited on site; only approved power boards with individual switches may be used.

## Overhead and underground services

Work near overhead power lines requires a documented exclusion zone based on voltage, and a spotter when plant operates within the observer zone. No crane, concrete pump, tipper or excavator may enter the no-go zone of an energised overhead line without written authority from the network operator.

## Live work prohibition

Work on energised electrical equipment is prohibited except for testing and fault finding by licensed electricians using safe test procedures. Circuits must be isolated, locked out and proven de-energised before work begins, following the site lockout tagout procedure.
